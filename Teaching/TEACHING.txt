The Teaching of Karma and Dharma
O Noble-born, if you have started to experience something strange, like voices in your head, or visions, then know that you have attained Enlightenment, and must live the life of a Monk, so as not to perish: answer and respond for everything, pray for, or atone for, if this was in fact, all the “sins” (misdeeds), correct all the mistakes, and not perish, noting each successful prayer (iteration of the practice of meditation out loud, and in action).


If you doubt Enlightenment and think that it is something like a virus, a movie, a video game, or cancer, as you yourself have invented, or “voices” have said, then try to conduct an experiment:
